OUR INTENTIONS

A Fairer, Greener Food System

We believe good food should never go to waste. MealMate makes surplus food easier to share while helping businesses reduce waste and support their local community.

Our Intentions

To create a community where good food is shared, not wasted.

Our Objectives

Reduce food waste, support local communities, and make food more accessible.

Lasting Impact

A cleaner environment, stronger communities, and a more sustainable future.

HOW IT WORKS

Simple Steps. Real Impact.

1

Businesses Share Food

Restaurants, cafés and local businesses post safe surplus food they have available.

2

Find & Reserve

Users browse available food, see pickup details, and reserve a portion.

3

Collect & Enjoy

Open Google Maps directions, collect during the pickup window, and help good food find a second home.

ABOUT US

Sharing Food. Supporting People.

MealMate connects people with restaurants, cafés and businesses that have safe surplus food available.

Instead of throwing good food away, businesses can make it available to their local community.
